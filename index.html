How it works

  1. Search: Enter a supported ticker such as RELIANCE or a company name such as Reliance Industries.
  2. Company lookup: The API checks its stock metadata to resolve the ticker, company name, exchange, and logo file.
  3. Logo delivery: The logo URL points to the image stored in this repository on GitHub. The page displays it beside the company name.
  4. Price and chart: The existing LTP endpoint fetches the quote and the chart endpoint loads historical prices. Quote availability depends on the price provider and supported symbol format.

API examples

Get the LTP:

GET /api/ltp?symbol=RELIANCE

Get LTP with company details and logo URL:

GET /api/ltp?symbol=RELIANCE&details=1

Get logo metadata as JSON:

GET /api/logo?symbol=RELIANCE&json=1

Display a logo through the API:

GET /api/logo?symbol=RELIANCE

Logo metadata and price-provider support are separate: a company may have a logo even when its quote is unavailable through the current LTP provider.
